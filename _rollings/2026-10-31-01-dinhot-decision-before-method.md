DinHot, start with the decision before the method
======

![The uneven trap array that frames DinHot's review problem](/images/projects/dinhot_pain.svg)

I am updating DinHot's product story around a practical question: under a stated setup, what does a design produce, how does the modeled device change it, and which metric makes two options comparable? The 0.02.001 release defines 14 synthetic cases and compares 24 methods using common tasks, seeds and metrics.

That makes the result easier to review. It does not make the result a measurement from a physical instrument. The bench, device response and camera feedback are modeled, and the product does not control an SLM or camera.
